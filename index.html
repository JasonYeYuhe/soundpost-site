Audio-first memory

Capture how a moment actually sounds — and let your future self open it.

Ten seconds of rain on the window. The café you loved. A voice you don't want to forget. Soundpost turns the sound of a moment into a tappable, mood-tinted waveform card — and can seal it to surface again on a date you choose.

Capture in seconds

Tap once. Record up to a minute of the sound around you — ambient, not staged. The whole habit is meant to be effortless.

The card is the keepsake

Each clip becomes a glanceable waveform card, tinted by the mood you give it, with an optional place and one line.

🕯️

Seal it for later

Choose a future date and Soundpost gently hides the capsule until then — a postcard you send forward in time.

🔒

Yours, on your device

No account. No feed. Your sounds stay on your phone. Nothing is sold, mined, or shown to anyone else.

What we promise — and what we won't pretend

Honesty over theater. Here is exactly how Soundpost behaves.

  • The seal is a gentle, honor-system seal — not encryption. It hides a capsule until its date, kept on your device. It is not a cryptographic lock and is not meant to defeat a determined person.
  • Recording can pick up other people's voices. Please be considerate of those around you when you capture a moment.
  • Capsules live on your device. In this version there is no cloud backup, so deleting the app removes your capsules. (Optional iCloud backup is on our roadmap.)
  • We never charge you to receive a past memory. Opening what you already captured is always free.
